Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

YT Drafts is a private tool its owner uses to upload and manage videos on a YouTube channel they manage. It uses YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, YT Drafts gets permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, and edit or delete videos on that YouTube channel. It doesn't read any other Google data.

How the data is handled

The sign-in token is stored only in the owner's computer keychain. Video titles, descriptions and files are sent only to YouTube. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke YT Drafts' access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page.

Google and YouTube

By using YT Drafts you are also using YouTube, which is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
